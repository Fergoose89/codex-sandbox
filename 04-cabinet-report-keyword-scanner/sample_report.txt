CABINET REPORT

Title: Budget Review and Capital Programme 2024/25
Date: 15 January 2024
Author: Director of Finance

1. PURPOSE OF REPORT

This report seeks Cabinet approval for the revised budget proposals for 2024/25,
including capital expenditure commitments and proposed savings measures.

2. RECOMMENDATIONS

Cabinet is recommended to:
a) Approve the revenue budget of £142 million for 2024/25.
b) Note the projected overspend of £2.3 million in Social Care.
c) Approve the capital programme totalling £18.5 million.
d) Endorse the proposed savings of £4.1 million across all departments.

3. BACKGROUND

The Council faces significant financial pressures due to rising demand for social
care services and inflationary pressures on contracts. The Director of Finance has
identified opportunities for efficiency savings and income generation.

4. FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

Total revenue budget: £142 million
Projected overspend: £2.3 million (Social Care)
Proposed savings: £4.1 million
Capital programme: £18.5 million
Reserves: £9.2 million (3.2% of net budget)

5. EQUALITY IMPACT

An equality impact assessment has been completed. The proposed savings have been
assessed for their impact on protected characteristics. No significant adverse
impacts have been identified, though some service reductions may affect vulnerable
residents.

6. RISK ASSESSMENT

Key risks include:
- Continued increase in social care demand beyond forecast
- Inflationary pressures exceeding budget assumptions
- Slippage in the capital programme delivery
- Uncertainty around government funding settlements

7. LEGAL IMPLICATIONS

The Council has a statutory duty to set a balanced budget. Failure to do so would
require intervention by the Secretary of State. Legal advice has been obtained
regarding the proposed savings programme.

8. CONCLUSION

Cabinet is asked to approve the budget proposals as set out in this report. The
Director of Finance is satisfied that the proposals represent a prudent and
sustainable financial strategy for the Council.
